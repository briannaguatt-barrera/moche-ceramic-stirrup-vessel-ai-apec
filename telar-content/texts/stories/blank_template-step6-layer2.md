title: "Go deeper"
---

Diving a bit deeper into Moche agriculture, we know that they heavily relied on corn to survive and prosper. The main reason the Moche were able to rely so much on corn was because they built intricate irrigation systems that allowed water to flow from nearby rivers into their fields or corn and other crops. The land the Moche lived on received very little rainfall, so they had to build architecture to support their living. Corn provided comfort for people by creating enough food for all, and showed power in who was able to provide the agriculture. In addition, corn was made to use Chica, which was a fermented drink often had during religious ceremonies and events. This further goes to show how much corn influenced Moche life, and why it was such an important aspect.
