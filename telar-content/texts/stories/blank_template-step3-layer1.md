The face on the Moche vessel represents Ai Apaec, a name given by scholars to a Moche deity shown in many artworks. His original name is unknown. On this vessel, Ai Apaec has large fangs and serpent-like features that make him look powerful and supernatural. His face stands out and helps identify him as an important religious figure. Some scholars connect Ai Apaec to sky and mountain beliefs because mountains were seen as sacred places that connected the earth to the heavens in Andean religion, but this idea is still debated. Some earlier researchers thought he was a supreme creator god, but newer studies suggest the Moche had several gods, and his importance is not fully understood
