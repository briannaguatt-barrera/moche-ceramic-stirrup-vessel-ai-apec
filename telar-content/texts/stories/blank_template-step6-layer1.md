Moche ceramics many times portray aspects of daily life, including farming, religion, and more. Corn was so important to Moche culture that it shows up in many ways in their pottery and other works of art. In the case of our vessel, we are shown a very specific part of Moche agriculture, corn. From what we know, the vessel shows the god Ai Apaec emerging from corn cobs, tying both religion and agriculture into one piece of art. It shows us how important these things are to their culture, and is in a way a visual record of Moche life.
